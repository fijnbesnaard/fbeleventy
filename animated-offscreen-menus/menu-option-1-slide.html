Slide In from Left

The off-screen menu slides in from the left using transform: translateX(-100%). Clean, predictable, widely understood.

Scrollbar jump is prevented with scrollbar-gutter: stable on the root element. A JS fallback measures the scrollbar width and applies it as padding-right on older browsers.

Scroll down to see that the page does not jump when the menu opens.
