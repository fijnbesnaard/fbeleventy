Clip-Path Circular Reveal

The menu reveals via clip-path: circle(), expanding from the exact center of the toggle button. The origin coordinates are calculated from the button's bounding rect so it always lines up perfectly, even after a window resize.

Browser support for clip-path circle animations is excellent across all modern browsers. The effect is GPU-composited and performs well even on mobile.

Open the menu — the circle expands outward from the button.
